  • Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, M4, Pro, Max, Ultra).

  • Standard USB-C or Micro-USB cable (for initial setup or wired mode).

2. ONE-TIME ANDROID PHONE CONFIGURATION
================================================================================

Step 1: Enable Developer Options
  1. Open your phone's "Settings" app.
  2. Scroll down and tap "About phone" (or "About device").
  3. Locate "Build number" (on some phones: Settings > About > Software information).
  4. Tap "Build number" rapidly 7 times until you see the message:
     "You are now a developer!"

Step 2: Enable USB Debugging
  1. Go back to main "Settings".
  2. Tap "System" or "Additional settings" > "Developer options".
  3. Find "USB debugging" and toggle it ON.

Step 3: Authorize Your Mac
  1. Plug your phone into your Mac with a USB cable.
  2. Unlock your phone screen.
  3. A prompt will appear: "Allow USB debugging from this computer?".
  4. Check the box "Always allow from this computer" and tap "Allow".

3. MANUFACTURER-SPECIFIC SETTINGS
================================================================================

Some phone manufacturers include extra security skins that require a toggle:

* SAMSUNG GALAXY (OneUI 5 / 6 / 7):
  - Lockscreen PIN: Samsung Knox blanks the video output while locked for
    privacy. Type your numeric PIN or password directly on your Mac keyboard
    and press Enter to unlock the phone!
  - Auto Blocker (OneUI 6+): If commands are blocked, check Settings > Security
    and privacy > Auto Blocker, and ensure USB debugging is allowed.

* XIAOMI / REDMI / POCO (HyperOS / MIUI):
  - Mouse & Keyboard Input: Xiaomi blocks simulated clicks by default!
    Go to Settings > Additional settings > Developer options:
      1. Turn ON "USB debugging".
      2. Turn ON "USB debugging (Security settings)"  <-- MANDATORY for clicks!
         (Note: Requires an active SIM card in the phone to toggle once).
      3. Turn ON "Install via USB" if you want drag-and-drop APK installs.

* OPPO / REALME / ONEPLUS (ColorOS / OxygenOS / RealmeUI):
  - OTG Auto-Off: ColorOS shuts off USB OTG after 10 minutes of inactivity.
    If the cable disconnects, re-enable "OTG connection" in Settings.
  - Permission Monitoring: In Developer options, enable
    "Disable permission monitoring" if clicks or taps are ignored.
  - Turn ON "Allow ADB debugging in charge only mode".

* VIVO / IQOO (FuntouchOS / OriginOS):
  - In Developer options, enable "USB debugging" and "Input simulation"
    (or "USB debugging (Security settings)").
  - Set default USB configuration to "File Transfer (MTP)".

* GOOGLE PIXEL / MOTOROLA / NOTHING:
  - Pure stock Android. Works immediately out of the box with just
    "USB debugging" enabled.

4. MAC INSTALLATION & FIRST LAUNCH
================================================================================

Open Terminal on your Mac and navigate into this folder:

  cd DroidMirror

Step 1: Run the Dependency Setup Script
  ./setup.sh
  (This verifies that adb and scrcpy-server.jar are present and executable).

Step 2: Launch Directly from Source
  swift run DroidMirror

Step 3: Build Standalone macOS App Bundle (Optional)
  ./bundle.sh
  (Builds and codesigns a native DroidMirror.app in the build/ folder).
  You can launch it anytime via:
    open build/DroidMirror.app

5. USING THE 3 CONNECTION SWITCHES
================================================================================

DroidMirror uses a deterministic button-driven connection model. It NEVER
flaps or switches networks in the background without your explicit click.

--- SWITCH 1: WIRED USB CABLE (Fastest, 16 Mbps, Zero Latency) ---
  1. Connect your phone via USB-C cable.
  2. Click the "1. Wired USB" button.
  3. Click "Connect via USB".
  4. TIP: Before going wireless, click "Prep for Wireless (Enable Port 5555)".
     This allows you to unplug the cable and use Switch 2 or 3!

--- SWITCH 2: STANDARD HOME WI-FI (Convenient, 6 Mbps, 60 FPS) ---
  1. Ensure your Mac and phone are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Click the "2. Home Wi-Fi" button.
  3. If your phone was plugged into USB, click "Detect IP" to autofill your
     phone's IP address (or type it manually, e.g. 192.168.1.105).
  4. Click "Connect & Switch".
  5. Android 11+ Pairing Code: If you don't have a USB cable, tap
     "Pair (Android 11+)", enter the pairing port and 6-digit code shown in
     phone's Wireless Debugging settings, and click Pair.

--- SWITCH 3: OFFLINE PHONE HOTSPOT (Field Work, No Router Needed) ---
  1. Turn ON "Personal Hotspot" on your phone.
  2. Connect your Mac's Wi-Fi to your phone's hotspot.
  3. In DroidMirror, click "3. Phone Hotspot".
  4. Click "Detect Gateway" (autofills gateway IP, typically 192.168.43.1).
  5. Click "Connect & Switch".

6. COMPACT FLOATING PHONE WINDOW (PICTURE-IN-PICTURE)
================================================================================

While streaming, you can shrink DroidMirror into a compact, phone-proportioned
floating window that stays on top of your workspace:

- Click the purple "Floating Window" button in the top bar, OR
- Press:  Shift + Command + F  (⇧⌘F)

The floating window provides:
• Physical smartphone screen proportions (~680pt height).
• Floats on top while you work in other apps.
• Full interactive mouse clicking, dragging, and typing.
• Compact header with live FPS counter, Back, Home, Power, and Expand buttons.

================================================================================

NAVIGATION & MOUSE:
  • Left-Click           -> Android Tap
  • Click & Drag         -> Android Swipe / Scroll
  • Right-Click          -> Android Back Button
  • Middle-Click         -> Android Home Button
  • Scroll-Wheel         -> Smooth vertical & horizontal scrolling

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS:

  • ⌘?  (Cmd+Shift+/)    -> Open Help & Universal Smartphone Guide
  • ⌘[                   -> Android Back Button
  • ⌃⌘H (Ctrl+Cmd+H)     -> Android Home Button
  • ⌃⌘S (Ctrl+Cmd+S)     -> Android App Switcher (Recents)
  • ⌃⌘P (Ctrl+Cmd+P)     -> Android Power Button (Turn screen on/off)
  • ⌃⌘O (Ctrl+Cmd+O)     -> Screen-Off Mirroring (Physical screen turns off,
                            mirroring continues to save battery)
  • ⌘C                   -> Copy text from phone to Mac pasteboard
  • ⌘V                   -> Paste text from Mac into phone

10. COMMON TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQS
================================================================================

Q: Phone says "Stream disconnected: socket disconnected" immediately on connect.
A: This usually means the phone's hardware video encoder rejected the bitrate
   or dimensions. DroidMirror Mac automatically detects this and steps down to
   Tier 2 (Safe 720p/30fps) or Tier 3 (Universal AOSP Software Encoder).
   Wait 1 second for the auto-recovery to reconnect.

Q: Screen is black when phone is locked (Samsung Galaxy).
A: Samsung Knox protects the PIN keypad with a black screen (FLAG_SECURE).
   Simply type your numeric PIN on your Mac keyboard and press Enter to unlock!

Q: Clicks and keyboard typing do nothing on Xiaomi / POCO / Redmi.
A: Xiaomi requires enabling "USB debugging (Security settings)" in Developer
   Options. You must have a SIM card inserted in the phone to toggle this once.

Q: "Port 5555 unreachable on 192.168.x.x".
A: Port 5555 is not enabled yet on the phone. Plug the phone in via USB cable
   once, select Switch 1, and click "Prep for Wireless (Enable Port 5555)".
   After that, you can unplug and use Wi-Fi or Hotspot!

Q: adb command gives "more than one device/emulator".
A: You have multiple phones connected. In DroidMirror Mac, use the top bar
   device selector dropdown to pick the exact phone you want to mirror.
